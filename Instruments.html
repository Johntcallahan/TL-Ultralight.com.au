CFI Avionic Instruments
New Technology
MEMS technology (Micro Electro Mechanical Sensor) has revolutionized the way aircraft instruments work. In the past, aircraft instruments such as accelerometers, gyroscopes, and pressure sensors were bulky and expensive to manufacture. However, with the advent of MEMS technology, these once bulky instruments now fit into a tiny chip that is only a few millimeters in size.
No more bellows or diaphrams. No more tap tap. Drift free for many years. Temperature compensated.
Available in 58mm or 80mm format.
Overall, MEMS technology has significantly improved aircraft safety, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness. Its use in aircraft instruments is an excellent example of how technology can revolutionize an industry and improve our lives.
If you have a smart phone, you are using several MEMS sensors. Airbags in cars are operated by MEMS sensors. Braking systems in modern cars cannot operate without MEMS. They are in use all around us and now are available to revolutionize avionic instruments.
Non certified, suitable for kit aircraft, LSA aircraft, Experimental or backup instruments.
